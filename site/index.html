Removal boundary

You control the local installation.

You can stop or remove the software at any time. Software-only removal can preserve selected data. Full removal deletes the identified assistant folder and can permanently remove memory, embeddings, training or adaptation state, ticket history, repositories, logs, credentials, and evidence.

Create and verify a backup before destructive removal. Deleted local state may not be recoverable.
